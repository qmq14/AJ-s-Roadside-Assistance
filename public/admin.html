Link your Square account

  1. Go to developer.squareup.com/apps and sign in with your Square account.
  2. Click + Create an application (any name, e.g. “Booking website”) and open it.
  3. On the Credentials page, copy the Application ID and Access token. Use Sandbox to test with fake cards first, then switch to Production.
  4. Open Locations in the left menu and copy your Location ID.
  5. Paste them below, click Save, then Test connection.
Sandbox test card: 4111 1111 1111 1111, any future expiry, CVV 111, ZIP 12345.

How to get a Gmail app password
  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account (myaccount.google.com → Security).
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Name it “Booking website”, click Create, and paste the 16-character password here.
